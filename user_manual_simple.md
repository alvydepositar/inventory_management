# Simple User Manual for Inventory Management System

## Introduction

### What is the System?
This is a web-based tool for managing products and stock in a retail business. It helps track items across different store locations.

### Purpose
The system allows you to view products, update stock levels, and check reports to keep inventory organized.

### What You Need
- A web browser like Chrome or Firefox.
- Login details from your administrator.
- Basic skills to click buttons and fill forms.

## Getting Started

### How to Log In
1. Open your browser and go to the application website.
2. Type your username or email in the username field.
3. Type your password in the password field.
4. Click the "Login" button.
5. You will go to the main dashboard or product list.

### Understanding the Interface
The system has a sidebar menu on the left for navigation. The main area shows tables and forms. Key sections:
- Dashboard: Quick overview of numbers.
- Product Catalogue: List of products.
- Manage Stocks: Update stock levels.
- Manage Users: (Admin only) Add or edit users.
- Stock History: View past changes.
- Account: Change your details.

[Annotated Screenshot: Sidebar Navigation - Arrows to each menu item with labels]

### User Roles
- **Admin**: Can do everything, including managing users.
- **Branch Manager**: Can update stock for their store.
- **User**: Can view products and stock, with limited changes.

## Daily Tasks

### Viewing the Dashboard
The dashboard shows key numbers like total products and stock alerts.
- Look for low stock warnings in red.
- Click on numbers to go to related pages.

[Annotated Screenshot: Dashboard - Arrows to count cards and alerts]

### Browsing Products
Go to Product Catalogue to see the product list.
- Use the search box to find items.
- Click on a product name for more details.

[Annotated Screenshot: Product Catalogue Page - Arrow to search box, label "Type to search"]

[Video: Browsing Products Walkthrough]

### Updating Stock
Go to Manage Stocks.
1. Select a branch from the dropdown.
2. Choose a product.
3. Click "Add Stock" or "Remove Stock".
4. Enter the quantity and notes.
5. Click "Save".

[Annotated Screenshot: Stock Management Page - Arrows to branch dropdown, product selection, and save button]

[Video: Updating Stock Walkthrough]

### Checking Stock History
Go to Stock History to see past changes.
- Filter by date or branch.
- View details like who made the change.

[Annotated Screenshot: Stock History Page - Arrow to filter options]

## Advanced Tasks

### Adding a Product (Admin/Manager)
1. Go to Product Catalogue.
2. Click "Add Product".
3. Fill in the form: Product ID, Name, Category, etc.
4. Click "Save".

[Annotated Screenshot: Add Product Form - Arrows to each field]

### Managing Users (Admin Only)
1. Go to Manage Users.
2. Click "Add User".
3. Fill in username, email, and role.
4. Click "Save".

[Annotated Screenshot: Add User Form - Arrows to fields]

### Viewing Reports
Use Dashboard for summaries or Stock History for details.
- Filter history by type or date.

[Annotated Screenshot: Filtered Stock History - Arrow to filter dropdown]

## Troubleshooting and Tips

### Common Problems
- **Can't log in?** Check your username and password. Contact admin if needed.
- **Page not loading?** Refresh the page or clear browser cache.
- **Can't save changes?** Make sure all required fields are filled.

### Helpful Tips
- Save often when entering data.
- Use filters to find information quickly.
- Check the dashboard daily for alerts.

## Glossary

- **Stock Level**: Current amount of a product.
- **Stock Movement**: A change to stock, like adding or removing.
- **Branch**: A store location.
- **Category**: A group for products, like "Paints".
- **Brand**: The maker of a product.
- **Supplier**: The company that provides products.
